# IsoStack Continuity Evidence

Purpose: hold dated evidence produced by the
[IsoStack Continuity And Operational Assurance Cycle](../00-overview/continuity-and-operational-assurance-cycle.md).

## Structure

```text
docs/continuity/
├── README.md
└── reviews/
    ├── CONTINUITY_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md
    └── YYYY/
        └── dated review and exercise records
```

## Rules

- Copy the template for each formal quarterly, six-monthly or annual review.
- Use `YYYY-MM-DD-<period-or-scenario>-<record-type>.md`.
- Mark planned records clearly; a future filename is not evidence of completion.
- Use links and factual, non-secret evidence.
- Do not record credentials, recovery codes, connection strings or unnecessary customer data.
- Do not use a continuity record to authorise development, deployment, rollback or database
  work.
- Route technical findings through the correct roadmap and implementation lifecycle.
- After sign-off, preserve the record and add later corrections as dated addenda.
